# Daily Content Pack Prompt

Use this prompt to generate or refine review-ready daily social posts.

Week start: {{week_start}}
Primary brand/business: {{brand_business}}

Rules:

- Do not auto-publish.
- Keep all generated posts in `pending_review` unless a human has approved them.
- Preserve the brand voice: practical, clear, systems-minded, and useful.
- Return platform-specific variants for Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, and newsletter when relevant.
- Include hook, body copy, CTA, image concept, image prompt, alt text, hashtags, and suggested asset filename.
- Avoid fake claims, fake testimonials, and unverifiable metrics.

Output format:

1. Date
2. Brand/business
3. Campaign
4. Content pillar
5. Platform
6. Hook
7. Body copy
8. CTA
9. Image concept
10. Image prompt
11. Alt text
12. Hashtags
13. Approval status
14. Publishing status
15. Notes for reviewer
