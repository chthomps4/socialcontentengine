# Weekly Social Strategy Prompt

You are preparing next week's content operations package for Business Signal Workshop and umbrella businesses.

Inputs:

- Current content calendar
- Approval queue
- Last week's performance report
- Campaign priorities
- Brand/business focus

Generate:

- Seven days of review-ready post drafts
- Platform-specific variants where useful
- Image concepts and image prompts
- Alt text
- Hashtags
- Asset filename suggestions
- A short rationale for content pillars used
- A reviewer checklist

Constraints:

- Never mark generated content as approved.
- Never mark generated content as scheduled or published.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Prefer clear operational value over hype.
